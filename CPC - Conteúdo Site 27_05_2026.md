# **Energy Healing Treatment for Pets**

**A gentle remote energy healing treatment to support your pet with comfort, balance, and care.**

Animals are deeply sensitive.

They feel changes in the home, emotional tension, grief, relocation, new family dynamics, vet visits, illness, aging, and subtle shifts in the environment around them.

Sometimes, this sensitivity appears in the body.

Other times, it appears in the small changes only someone who truly loves them would notice: their mood, sleep, appetite, restlessness, fear, distance, or the feeling that they are simply not fully themselves.

The Energy Healing Treatment for Pets was created to support your pet at this deeper layer.

Using your pet’s photo and intake form, I connect with their energetic field and create a personalized remote treatment using Reiki, radiesthesia, pendulum work, energetic clearing, grounding, balancing, and subtle energy healing techniques.

This is not a single session that begins and ends in one moment.

The treatment unfolds over 2 to 7 days, giving your pet’s energy time to receive, respond, and integrate gently.

Throughout the process, you receive updates on what I am sensing, what is shifting, what still feels sensitive, and what your pet may need energetically.

At the end, I read your pet again and send a closing report with the evolution of the treatment, key insights, and any recommended next steps.
